5. PARTIES

parties description.

Read the parties in their original sequence.

Extract their personal name and their father's or husband's name.

occupation or other details. Exclude all such details from
Name and Relation Name.

Identify where the Executant's description ends and the next

CLAIMANTS:

Classify parties using the actual deed descriptions and legal
context, not merely their order of appearance.

Party Type must be exactly "Executant" or "Claimant".

For all parties:
- Name: personal name only.
- Relation Name: father's or husband's name only.
- Remove titles, honorifics, caste, addresses and descriptions.
- Transliterate Hindi or Urdu names into English faithfully.
- Never merge different people or omit identifiable parties.
